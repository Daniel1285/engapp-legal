Privacy Policy

EngApp · Last updated 6 October 2026

EngApp is an English-learning app for Hebrew speakers. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Your age group, not your date of birth. Before any sign-in option is shown, every new user is asked their date of birth on a neutral screen. Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only when the user chooses to use the speaking practice feature.

Audio is processed temporarily using the speech-recognition service provided by the device's operating system. Depending on the device and operating system, this processing may involve Apple's or Google's speech-recognition services.

We do not store voice recordings on our servers or in Supabase. The recognized text is used only to provide the requested learning feedback and is not intended to be stored permanently.

Advertising

Users without a Premium subscription see ads supplied by Google AdMob. Which ads you see depends on your age:

  • Child, teen, or age unknown: non‑personalized ads only. Your ad identifier is not used to build an advertising profile. Requests are tagged as child‑directed and/or under the age of consent, as applicable, and limited to a lower maximum ad content rating.
  • Adult: personalized ads only if you consent when asked. You may decline and still use the app; you will see non‑personalized ads instead.
  • Premium and Teacher Premium subscribers: no ads at all.

Google's handling of ad data is described in Google's Privacy & Terms.

Age groups

EngApp can be used by learners of all ages. When you first open the app you are asked your date of birth on a neutral screen (no answer is pre-selected). It is used only on your device to work out one of three age groups — child, teen or adult — and is then discarded. The age group is stored with the account and cannot be changed afterwards, so the protections that apply to an account cannot be removed by re‑answering.

For a child or teen account, and for any account whose age group is not known, the following applies:

  • Non‑personalized ads only. No advertising profile is built, and ad requests are tagged as child‑directed and/or under the age of consent, with a limit on the ad content rating.
  • No tracking prompt (iOS) and no advertising consent form is shown.
  • No rewarded ads, and no purchase prompts that use urgency or countdown pressure.
  • For a child account specifically, we do not show unsolicited "upgrade to Premium" prompts at all — the subscription screen is only ever reached if you navigate to it yourself.
  • For a child account specifically, no push notification token is collected and no reminder notifications are sent.
  • If a child account is used to start a purchase, the app itself first asks for confirmation that the person completing it is 18 or older and the account's parent or legal guardian, before the purchase can proceed. Apple or Google may separately apply their own parental approval requirements (for example Ask to Buy or Google Play approvals), depending on the account and family settings.

If you are a parent or guardian and want to review or delete your child’s data, contact engappsupport@gmail.com and we will act on it. You can also delete the account in the app (Settings → Delete Account).

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share only what these services need to function:

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics, tagged with an internal account identifier only.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • RevenueCat, with Apple and Google — subscription management, keyed by an internal user identifier.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications.
  • Apple and Google — sign‑in, and (through your device's operating system) speech recognition during speaking practice, as described in Microphone and speech recognition.

If you join a class, your display name and learning progress are visible to that class's teacher. That is the purpose of a class. If you rank in the top 100 on the monthly leaderboard, your display name and points are visible to all EngApp users for that month.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correction of it, or deletion of your account and everything in it. Write to engappsupport@gmail.com. You can delete your account yourself in the app (Settings → Delete Account); see Delete Your Account for what is removed and what is kept.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU/UK GDPR, the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, or the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem they describe.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects you, tell you in the app.
