Privacy Policy

EngApp · Last updated 30 September 2026

EngApp is an English-learning app for Hebrew speakers aged 13 and up. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

EngApp is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users under 13 are prohibited from using EngApp or creating an account. Before any sign-in option is shown, every new user is asked their date of birth on a neutral screen; anyone under 13 is stopped there — no account, no sign-in, and no data of any kind is collected or stored for them. EngApp does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Who we are

For any question about this policy or your data, contact engappsupport@gmail.com.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, or browsing history. Microphone access occurs only when a speaking exercise begins, and it is used to provide speech recognition features.

Advertising

Users without a Premium subscription see ads supplied by Google AdMob. Which ads you see depends on your age:

  • Under 18, or age unknown: non‑personalized ads only. Your ad identifier is not used to build an advertising profile. Requests are tagged as child‑directed and/or under the age of consent, as applicable.
  • 18 and over: personalized ads only if you consent when asked. You may decline and still use the app; you will see non‑personalized ads instead.
  • Teachers and Premium subscribers: no ads at all.

Google's handling of ad data is described in Google's Privacy & Terms.

Users under 18

EngApp does not permit anyone under 13 to create an account, sign in, or use the app. This is enforced before sign-in: a neutral date-of-birth screen runs first, and Apple/Google sign-in is only offered afterwards if the answer is 13 or older. If it isn't, nothing further happens — no account is created, and no data is collected or stored.

Users aged 13–17 have an account, but with stricter protections than an adult account:

  • Non‑personalized ads only. No advertising profile is built.
  • No consent or tracking prompt is ever shown to a user under 18.
  • No pressure to purchase. Users under 18 may be subject to parental approval requirements for purchases, or family account requirements imposed by Apple or Google, depending on their account and family settings.
  • We ask for a date of birth once, before the account exists, and store it once the account is created. It cannot be changed afterwards, so the protections that apply to an account cannot be removed by re‑answering.

If you are a parent or guardian and want to review or delete your teen's data, contact engappsupport@gmail.com and we will act on it.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share only what these services need to function:

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • RevenueCat, with Apple and Google — subscription management.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications.
  • Apple and Google — sign‑in.

If you join a class, your display name and learning progress are visible to that class's teacher. That is the purpose of a class.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correction of it, or deletion of your account and everything in it. Write to engappsupport@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your profile, progress, custom words and class membership.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU/UK GDPR, the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, or the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem they describe.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects you, tell you in the app.
