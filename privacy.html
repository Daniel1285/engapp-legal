Privacy Policy

EngApp · Last updated 28 August 2026

EngApp is an English-learning app for Hebrew speakers of all ages, including children. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Who we are

EngApp is operated by E&G. For any question about this policy or your data, contact engappsupport@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and account identifier, via Sign in with Apple or GoogleTo create and secure your account. We never see or store your password.
Display name you chooseShown to you, and to your teacher and classmates if you join a class.
Date of birthTo determine which advertising and privacy rules apply to your account. See Children.
English level, learning progress, words marked learned, review schedule, streak, custom words and foldersTo run the app and pick what to teach you next.
Class membership, join code, teacher/school roleOnly if you join a class or are a teacher.
Push notification token, device platform, languageTo send study reminders. Only if you allow notifications.
Subscription status, product purchased, transaction identifierTo unlock Premium features. We never receive your card or payment details — those stay with Apple or Google.
Advertising identifier, IP address, general device informationCollected by Google AdMob to show ads to users who are not subscribed. See Advertising.
Crash and error diagnostics: platform, app version, error messageTo find and fix bugs.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, or browsing history. The microphone is used only for speaking practice, on your device, when you start it.

Advertising

Users without a Premium subscription see ads supplied by Google AdMob. Which ads you see depends on your age:

  • Under 18, or age unknown: non‑personalized ads only. Your ad identifier is not used to build an advertising profile. Requests are tagged as child‑directed and/or under the age of consent, as applicable.
  • 18 and over: personalized ads only if you consent when asked. You may decline and still use the app; you will see non‑personalized ads instead.
  • Teachers and Premium subscribers: no ads at all.

Google's handling of ad data is described in Google's Privacy & Terms.

Children

EngApp is used by children, and their accounts are treated more strictly than adult accounts:

  • Non‑personalized ads only. No advertising profile is built.
  • No consent or tracking prompt is ever shown to a child.
  • No pressure to purchase. If a child taps to subscribe, the payment request goes to a parent through Apple's Ask to Buy or Google Play's parental approval — the child never completes a payment inside the app.
  • We ask for a date of birth once, in neutral wording, and store it. It cannot be changed afterwards, so the protections that apply to an account cannot be removed by re‑answering.

If you are a parent or guardian and want to review or delete your child's data, contact engappsupport@gmail.com and we will act on it.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share only what these services need to function:

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • RevenueCat, with Apple and Google — subscription management.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications.
  • Apple and Google — sign‑in.

If you join a class, your display name and learning progress are visible to that class's teacher. That is the purpose of a class.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correction of it, or deletion of your account and everything in it. Write to engappsupport@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your profile, progress, custom words and class membership.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU/UK GDPR, the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, or the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We apply the protections described here to all users regardless of location.

Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem they describe.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects you, tell you in the app.
